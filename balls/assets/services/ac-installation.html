Why It Matters

What Sets a Proper Ottawa AC Installation Apart

Ottawa's high summer humidity means an incorrectly sized or improperly charged AC system will run continuously, fail to dehumidify, and drive electricity bills to uncomfortable levels. Proper installation starts with load calculation, not guesswork.

Correct Cooling Load Calculation

We calculate your home's exact cooling load using Manual J methodology — accounting for Ottawa's peak design temperature, your home's orientation, window area, insulation R-values, and internal heat gains. Oversized units cool too fast, cycle off without dehumidifying, and leave the air damp and uncomfortable.

Brazed Refrigerant Lines & Proper Charge

We braze copper refrigerant line sets and verify the system charge with calibrated gauges against manufacturer superheat and subcooling specifications. An incorrectly charged system will underperform, ice up, and fail its compressor prematurely — often within two to three seasons.

Written Flat-Rate — No Hidden Fees

Your written quote includes the condenser, evaporator coil, copper line set, electrical disconnect, concrete pad, labour, and permit. The number on that document is the final amount you pay — no surprise add-ons after the equipment is installed.

Installation Process

How AC Installation Works With Our Team

A transparent, four-step process from your first call to the first cool day.

01

Cooling Load Assessment & Quote

We evaluate your home's heat gain from all sources, assess duct condition and airflow, and inspect the existing electrical capacity. You receive a written flat-rate quote covering all materials, labour, permit, and disposal before any work is authorized.

02

Equipment Selection & Permit

We source the correctly-rated condenser and matching evaporator coil, pull the required building permit, and schedule your installation. We do not carry oversized equipment to upsell — we carry what the calculation requires.

03

Precision Installation

We set the condenser on a level concrete pad, braze and pressure-test refrigerant lines, make all electrical connections, and perform a refrigerant charge verification using manufacturer specifications before commissioning the system.

04

System Test & Homeowner Walkthrough

We run a full cooling cycle test, verify supply temperature differential across the coil, and walk you through thermostat programming and filter maintenance. Old equipment is removed and your property is left clean.
