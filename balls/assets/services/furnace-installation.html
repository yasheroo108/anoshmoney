Gas Furnace Installation & Replacement in Ottawa

From single-stage to fully modulating variable-speed systems, we install the right furnace for your Ottawa home's layout, age, and duct configuration — not simply the highest-margin unit in our truck.

High-Efficiency Gas Furnace (96% AFUE)

Today's top-tier condensing gas furnaces convert up to 96% of fuel into usable heat — a significant upgrade over the 60–70% AFUE units found in Ottawa homes built before 1995. We perform a Manual J heat load calculation to correctly size the unit, preventing the short-cycling and cold spots caused by improper over-sizing.

Why Choose Us

Proper Furnace Installations, Not Quick Swaps

Many Ottawa HVAC companies treat furnace replacements as a unit swap — disconnect the old, connect the new. Our technicians perform proper Manual J load calculations, combustion air evaluations, and full ductwork inspections on every job.

Correct Sizing Every Time

An oversized furnace short-cycles, causing uneven temperatures, excess humidity, and premature heat exchanger failure. We calculate the right BTU output for your home's exact square footage, insulation, and window layout — never guessing.

TSSA Licensed Gas Technicians Only

Every gas connection, venting configuration, and combustion test is performed by a TSSA-licensed gas fitter with full liability insurance. No subcontractors, no apprentices working unsupervised.

Written Flat-Rate Before We Start

Your written quote includes the unit, labour, venting materials, permit fees, and old furnace disposal. The number on that document is the final invoice — no exceptions.

Installation Process

How Our Furnace Installation Works

A clear, four-step process from quote to comfortable home.

01

Assessment & Heat Load Calculation

We measure your home, assess duct condition, inspect venting, and perform a formal Manual J heat load calculation. This determines the exact BTU capacity required — the most critical step most Ottawa companies skip.

02

Written Quote & Equipment Sourcing

You receive a flat-rate quote including equipment, labour, venting, permits, and disposal. We source the correctly-sized unit — not the highest margin one — and schedule your installation within the week.

03

Safe Removal & Precision Install

We decommission and remove the old furnace, make all code-compliant gas, electrical, and venting connections, and run a full combustion test with calibrated instruments before declaring the system operational.

04

Thermostat Setup & Homeowner Walkthrough

We configure your thermostat, explain filter change intervals, and walk you through your warranty documentation. Old equipment is hauled away and your mechanical room is left clean.
