Furnace Replacement in Barrhaven

Barrhaven's older homes — particularly those built in the late 1990s and early 2000s in areas like Pheasant Run and Craig Henry — are frequently on furnaces that are past their reliable service window. A new 96% AFUE condensing furnace not only heats more evenly but typically reduces your natural gas bill by 25–35% compared to a mid-efficiency unit from that era. We assess your existing ductwork, verify gas line capacity, and confirm the correct venting configuration before installation day so there are no surprises.

Air Conditioner & Heat Pump Installation

Barrhaven summers bring Ottawa's full humidity load, and a correctly sized central air conditioner is the difference between a comfortable home and a system that runs constantly without properly dehumidifying. We calculate your cooling load from first principles, select the right condenser tonnage, and complete a full refrigerant charge verification before we leave. We also install cold-climate heat pumps for homeowners looking to reduce gas consumption — these operate efficiently at Ottawa temperatures as low as -25°C when paired with a gas backup.

Water Heater Service in Barrhaven

Tankless water heater conversions are one of the most popular upgrades we complete for Barrhaven households. Moving from a conventional storage tank to an on-demand Navien or Rinnai system eliminates standby heat loss and frees up mechanical room space. For households that want straightforward reliability, we also offer same-week gas tank water heater replacements with proper venting, T&P valve installation, and full code compliance — including disposal of the old unit.
