Your Local HVAC Experts in Manotick

That diversity in housing stock means HVAC needs here are rarely one-size-fits-all. Nepean Home Services has worked extensively throughout Manotick and the surrounding rural communities of Greely, Kars, Osgoode, and North Gower, giving us direct experience with the range of mechanical systems found in this area.

Furnace Replacement in Manotick

Manotick's older village core includes homes with a wide variety of heating configurations — some have conventional forced-air systems with older mid-efficiency furnaces, while others rely on boilers, electric baseboards, or propane where natural gas isn't available. When natural gas is on-site, upgrading to a 96% AFUE condensing furnace delivers significant savings on utility bills and far more consistent comfort throughout the home. We assess your ductwork condition, gas supply capacity, and combustion air requirements before the installation date so there are no day-of complications.

Air Conditioning & Heat Pump Installation in Manotick

Manotick's larger lots and mature tree cover can make cooling load calculations less predictable than in dense suburban subdivisions — shading patterns, south-facing windows, and home orientation all influence the right AC size. We perform a proper cooling load assessment and select the condenser tonnage that will keep humidity under control while maintaining efficiency at part-load conditions. For Manotick homeowners interested in reducing gas dependency, cold-climate heat pumps are now a practical year-round solution, operating efficiently even at Ottawa's coldest winter temperatures when paired with a gas backup system.

Water Heater Service in Manotick

Homes in Manotick that draw from a private well should pay close attention to water heater sediment buildup and anode rod condition — mineral content in well water accelerates tank degradation. When a replacement is due, we can assess whether a tankless Navien or Rinnai system is appropriate for your water quality and household demand. For properties where propane is the primary fuel, we also install and service propane-fired tankless and tank water heaters with full compliance to TSSA standards and local building codes.
