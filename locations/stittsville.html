Furnace Replacement in Stittsville

Many Stittsville homes built between 2000 and 2015 are equipped with mid-efficiency furnaces rated around 80% AFUE. Upgrading to a modern 96% AFUE two-stage condensing furnace can reduce your natural gas consumption by 25–35% annually while delivering noticeably more even heat distribution throughout larger floor plans. We assess your existing ductwork for balance and static pressure, verify the gas supply line capacity, and confirm the correct venting configuration — whether that means a new PVC concentric vent kit through the side wall or a liner for an existing masonry chase. Nothing is left to assumptions on installation day.

Air Conditioner & Heat Pump Installation

Stittsville's summers bring the same heat and humidity load as the rest of Ottawa, and a correctly sized central air conditioner is critical to keeping indoor comfort levels in check without running your system around the clock. We calculate your home's cooling load from scratch — not from the old condenser's nameplate — and select a unit that will properly dehumidify your living space, not just lower the temperature. For homeowners interested in year-round efficiency, cold-climate heat pumps are an increasingly practical choice in west Ottawa. Modern units operate effectively at temperatures down to -25°C, providing substantial heating capacity with reduced reliance on natural gas when paired with a compatible furnace backup.

Water Heater Service in Stittsville

Stittsville households with multiple bathrooms and busy morning schedules are ideal candidates for a tankless water heater upgrade. Moving from a conventional storage tank to an on-demand system like a Navien NPE or Rinnai RU series unit eliminates standby heat loss, provides continuous hot water regardless of simultaneous demand, and frees up valuable mechanical room floor space. For families who prefer the straightforward reliability of a traditional tank, we offer same-week gas and electric water heater replacements with full compliance to current Ontario gas code requirements — including proper venting, T&P valve installation, and complete removal of the old unit at no additional charge.
