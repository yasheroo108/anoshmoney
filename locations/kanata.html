Furnace Replacement in Kanata

Kanata's older homes — particularly those in Bridlewood, South Marchfield, and Beaverbrook — are commonly on furnaces that were installed in the mid-to-late 1990s. These units, if still functioning, are typically running at 60–70% AFUE, producing significantly more carbon monoxide risk than modern condensing equipment and costing considerably more to operate each winter. Replacing them with a 96% AFUE unit involves new PVC venting, condensate management, and often a gas line verification — all of which we include in the flat-rate quote. Newer Kanata builds in Morgan's Grant and Fairwinds more often need like-for-like replacements on two-stage or modulating equipment, which our technicians are fully certified to handle.

Air Conditioning Installation in Kanata

Kanata's western location puts it slightly further from the river humidity that affects Gloucester and Orléans, but Ottawa summers still demand a properly sized AC system. Kanata homes — particularly the larger executive builds in Katimavik and Glen Cairn — often require higher-tonnage condensers than a technician might assume at a glance. We calculate the correct cooling load before any equipment is ordered. We also install cold-climate heat pumps as a complete replacement for aging central AC systems, which qualifies many Kanata homeowners for available federal and provincial rebate programs.

Water Heater Service in Kanata

Kanata has a high concentration of tankless water heater installations compared to other Ottawa areas, driven by the larger home sizes that demand more simultaneous hot water output and the tech-savvy homeowner base that has researched the efficiency benefits. We install Navien, Rinnai, and Bosch tankless systems with proper gas line sizing, correct venting configuration, and annual descaling guidance. For tank replacements, we offer same or next-day service across all Kanata neighbourhoods with no travel premium.
