Your Local HVAC Experts in Nepean

Nepean has one of the most varied housing landscapes in Ottawa. From the post-war bungalows along Merivale Road and the established two-storeys of Centrepointe and Knoxdale to the newer townhomes of Bells Corners and Barrhaven's northern edge, the range of HVAC equipment across Nepean is equally broad. Nepean Home Services has deep roots in this part of the city — and our name reflects that. We've been installing and replacing furnaces, air conditioners, and water heaters for Nepean homeowners for over 15 years.

Nepean's housing stock skews older in many pockets, which means a significant number of homes are running furnaces that are well past their expected service life. A 20-year-old mid-efficiency furnace operating at 80% AFUE is costing you significantly more to run than a modern 96% AFUE condensing unit — and it's far more likely to fail during the coldest stretch of an Ottawa winter. Our TSSA-licensed gas fitters assess your full heating system before quoting a replacement, ensuring the right size and configuration for your home.

Furnace Replacement in Nepean

Nepean's older neighbourhoods — particularly those developed through the 1960s, 70s, and 80s along corridors like Baseline Road, Merivale, and Woodroffe Avenue — are home to a large number of aging furnaces. Many of these units have already required significant repairs and are operating inefficiently. Replacing a worn mid-efficiency furnace with a properly sized 96% AFUE condensing model typically delivers heating bill reductions of 25–35% while providing far more even heat distribution throughout the home. We handle full ductwork inspection, gas line verification, and proper condensate drainage as part of every installation.

Air Conditioning & Heat Pump Installation

Ottawa's summer humidity is felt as much in Nepean as anywhere in the city, and the right air conditioner size is critical for effective dehumidification — not just temperature reduction. An oversized unit will cool quickly but short-cycle, leaving excess moisture in the air. We calculate your home's actual cooling load and select a unit that will run efficiently at Ottawa's summer design conditions. We also install cold-climate heat pumps, which are increasingly popular in Nepean as homeowners look to reduce natural gas consumption while maintaining reliable year-round heating and cooling.

Water Heater Replacement in Nepean

Water heater failures don't announce themselves in advance — most Nepean homeowners discover the problem when they step into a cold shower or find water on the utility room floor. We maintain stock of the most common residential tank sizes for gas and electric systems, allowing us to complete most replacements same-day or next-day when you call early. For households ready to move to tankless, we install Navien and Rinnai on-demand systems with proper gas line sizing, venting upgrades, and full code compliance. Old units are disconnected, removed, and disposed of as part of every job.
