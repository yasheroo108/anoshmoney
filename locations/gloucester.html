Furnace Replacement in Gloucester

Gloucester's older residential areas have a high concentration of homes still running mid-efficiency furnaces from the 1990s — units with 60–70% AFUE ratings that have long been surpassed by the current standard of 96% AFUE condensing equipment. Upgrading to a high-efficiency gas furnace in a Gloucester home typically involves transitioning from a B-vent chimney configuration to a sealed PVC combustion air and exhaust system. Our technicians assess your home's specific layout and determine the correct venting path before the installation day, so the work is completed in a single visit with no return trips.

Air Conditioning Service in Gloucester

Many Gloucester homes have central air conditioners approaching or past the 15-year mark. A failing condenser that runs continuously without properly cooling or dehumidifying the home is a sign of refrigerant loss or an undersized system. When we install a replacement, we calculate the correct cooling load for your specific square footage, insulation, and window orientation — not simply matching the capacity of what came out. Properly sized AC systems cycle correctly, control humidity effectively, and last longer.

Water Heater Upgrades in Gloucester

Gloucester homeowners frequently contact us after discovering a slow leak under an aging storage tank. In Gloucester's older home stock, many tanks are mounted on finished floors in lower levels, where even a minor leak causes significant water damage. We prioritize prompt replacement scheduling for these situations and carry common residential tank sizes for same-day or next-day service. We also complete tankless water heater conversions, which are particularly effective in Gloucester homes where the mechanical room is in a finished basement and reclaiming floor space matters.
