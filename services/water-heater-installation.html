Why Act Now

Signs Your Ottawa Home Needs a New Water Heater

Most Ottawa homeowners wait until a tank fails — often causing water damage to finished basements. Proactive replacement is less disruptive and typically more affordable than emergency service.

Unit is 10+ Years Old

The average lifespan of a storage tank water heater in Ottawa's hard water conditions is 10–12 years. Beyond that, sediment buildup and anode rod corrosion dramatically reduce heating efficiency and accelerate tank failure.

Rust-Coloured or Smelly Water

Discoloured or metallic-smelling hot water typically indicates advanced interior tank corrosion. This cannot be resolved through flushing — a full replacement is the correct and safest resolution.

Our Installation Process

How Water Heater Installation Works

A transparent, four-step process from first call to final sign-off.

01

On-Site Assessment & Quote

We evaluate your existing equipment, gas line capacity, venting configuration, and available clearances. You receive a written flat-rate quote before any work is authorized.

02

Permit & Parts Preparation

Where required, we pull the appropriate building permit and source the correctly-sized water heater for your home's demand profile — no upselling, just proper sizing.

03

Safe Removal & Clean Installation

We safely drain and disconnect the old unit, make all necessary gas, venting, and plumbing connections, and test the new system under full operating pressure before we leave.

04

Final Inspection & Walkthrough

We walk you through the new system's operation, temperature settings, and annual maintenance requirements. Old equipment is fully removed from your property at no extra charge.
